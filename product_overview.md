## Permanent Leak Detection Monitoring

Compact, permanently installed correlating leak noise loggers featuring direct NB-IoT data communication for daily automatic leak detection in water distribution networks

• 95% connectivity rate from underground chamber to the cloud

• No drilling of holes required, even through cast iron lids

• Daily automatic 1ms time synchronization of all loggers for highest leak pinpointing accuracy (full correlation)

• Advanced internal sensors: 3D motion sensor to detect theft or misplacement, humidity and temperature sensors, and a charge gauge for precise battery life prediction

• Easy installation with ZONESCAN INSTALL Android app
